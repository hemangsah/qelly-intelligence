# Qelly writing standard

## Voice

Qelly is precise, evidence-led, calm, direct, professional, and transparent. Analytical screens state what is observed, what is derived, what is uncertain, and what is unavailable.

## Required truth labels

- **Live** — within the declared freshness threshold from an enabled provider.
- **Delayed** — received on a known delay; show the delay.
- **Estimated** — modelled or inferred; show methodology and limitation.
- **Derived** — transformed from named inputs; show methodology version.
- **Demo · not live** — deterministic illustrative record.
- **Fallback · not live** — substitute or last-known-good record; show the reason.
- **Stale** — beyond the declared freshness threshold; show observation time.
- **Unavailable** — no trustworthy value; use an em dash instead of zero.

Never describe demo, fallback, fixture, estimated, or stale data as live.

## Financial and AI boundaries

Avoid guaranteed outcomes, unsupported superlatives, fake urgency, vague AI claims, “100% accurate,” “risk-free,” “best investment,” and prescriptive buy/sell language. A favourable move is not a recommendation.

AI output must state:

- cited evidence and source links where available;
- observation and generation times;
- freshness, confidence, uncertainty, and limitations;
- provider disagreement;
- model or service identity when appropriate;
- that unsupported financial advice is not provided.

## Interface copy

- Buttons use a verb and object: “Inspect source,” “Export evidence,” “Create alert.”
- Empty states explain why the state exists and offer the smallest recovery action.
- Errors identify the failed dependency without exposing secrets or internals.
- Permission states distinguish authentication from authorization.
- Dates and times include timezone where ambiguity matters.
- Units and currencies remain next to values.
- Identifiers and hashes may truncate visually only when the complete value remains accessible.

## Public narrative

Public storytelling may be expressive but cannot become promotional ambiguity. Approved original narrative examples include:

> Markets create noise. Qelly finds structure.

> Observations become evidence. Evidence becomes intelligence.

> Intelligence becomes a decision. Every decision keeps its history.
