QELLY GLOBAL PUBLIC BETA

Public beta notice

Qelly is a free public beta, not an enterprise production service. Features may change, free services may pause or reach quotas, and connected cloud capabilities remain unavailable until their real deployments are authorized and verified.

What is available

Qelly provides deterministic calculators, formula and indicator libraries, local saved calculations, transparent methodology, data-source labels and safe degraded modes. The deterministic layer is designed to remain useful without an account and during network or provider failure.

Connected capabilities

Authentication, cloud synchronization, protected feedback writes and live providers are enabled only after a real authorized service passes security, isolation, availability and product-truth gates. A mock, fixture, configured URL or API contract is never presented as a live service.

Free-tier limitations

The public beta uses legitimate free tiers and a free platform subdomain. Qelly does not attach a payment method, purchase a domain, enable paid add-ons or permit billable overages. Near a quota limit, Qelly may prefer cached data, suspend nonessential writes or return to local-only mode.

Not included

Qelly does not trade, hold assets, accept deposits, process withdrawals or transfers, sign wallet transactions, handle private keys or seed phrases, execute through brokers or exchanges, make autonomous investment decisions, or provide personalized financial advice.

Feedback

Public-beta users can report defects, accessibility issues, privacy concerns and data-quality problems through the support page. Do not submit passwords, tokens, private keys, seed phrases or sensitive financial inputs.
