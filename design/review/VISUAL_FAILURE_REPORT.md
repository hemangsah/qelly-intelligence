# PR #11 rejected visual assessment

Status: rejected by the user; not approved.

## Failures in the previous interface

- Burgundy was used as atmosphere across the canvas instead of as an identity accent.
- Gradients repeated across cards, buttons, chart fills, logo, status areas, and backgrounds.
- Repeated rounded rectangles created a generic dashboard grid with weak hierarchy.
- Typography overused bold weight and did not separate editorial, application, numeric, and evidence roles.
- Unicode glyphs and improvised symbols made navigation feel temporary.
- The market chart read as a synthetic line/sine-wave illustration rather than realistic OHLC software.
- The ranking table was pushed below an oversized chart and treated as secondary.
- Motion was limited to decoration rather than route continuity, table interaction, drawers, sheets, and charts.
- Mobile stacked desktop modules and remained visually heavy.
- Static/demo truth labels repeated too often and competed with market content.
- The product lacked CoinGlass-level derivatives density, CoinMarketCap-level discovery breadth, and WorldQuant-level editorial restraint.

## Required correction

The Phase 0 reset replaces rather than polishes the rejected direction: neutral institutional materials, rare gradients, a compact shell, SVG icons, table-first composition, realistic deterministic OHLC, Discovery/Terminal/Research modes, premium controlled motion, and purpose-built mobile behavior.

No prior visual-fidelity percentage remains valid. Visual approval belongs only to the user after reviewing the regenerated draft-PR artifact.
