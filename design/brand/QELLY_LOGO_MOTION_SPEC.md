# Qelly Logo Motion Specification

The opening experience resolves the Q symbol, trajectory, nodes and wordmark in approximately 1,180 ms. It is session-aware, never repeats on route navigation, and can be skipped by interaction.

Reduced motion resolves in 120 ms without transforms. Repeat visits in the same session skip the opening. No continuous loops, autoplay sound, video, blur stack or long artificial delay are permitted.
