# Qelly Intelligence design principles

1. **Evidence travels with the value.** Provider, time, freshness, quality, confidence, and fallback are part of the content—not optional decoration.
2. **Density follows the task.** Public pages breathe; analytical workspaces stay stable and dense; research remains readable; operations stay calm.
3. **Burgundy identifies Qelly, not every surface.** The signature gradient anchors chrome and major evidence moments. Solid porcelain, white, graphite, and burgundy-black carry the work.
4. **Color never carries meaning alone.** Direction, risk, freshness, and fallback pair color with symbols, labels, structure, or pattern.
5. **Personas change hierarchy.** The six modes alter density, horizon, ordering, alert posture, motion, and terminology while keeping risk and provenance visible.
6. **Motion explains.** Motion may show navigation, causality, freshness, focus, or loading. It is bounded, non-looping by default, and removable without loss of meaning.
7. **Failure is a designed state.** Empty, no-results, partial, stale, fallback, demo, permission, maintenance, and offline states preserve context and recovery guidance.
8. **Progressive disclosure beats giant menus and card walls.** The shell reveals one product domain and its contextual routes at a time.
9. **Numbers are machine-readable and human-legible.** Financial values use tabular numerals, aligned decimals, explicit units, and full-value access.
10. **Static preview and production remain separate.** Demo records are deterministic, mutation-free, secret-free, and clearly labelled. Production contracts remain strict.
