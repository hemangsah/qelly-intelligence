# Part 22 - Sovereign Experience Design System

## Permanent decision

The signature Qelly gradient is locked for future releases unless the founder explicitly revokes it. It is not a theme setting. It is the stable brand layer beneath every persona.

## Persona model

Personas represent market behaviour and task intensity:

1. Scalper Velocity - high tempo, compact density, immediate market signals.
2. Investor Compound - calm motion, generous reading rhythm, long-horizon evidence.
3. Aggressive Alpha - catalyst and volatility emphasis with low-glare surfaces.
4. Quant Operator - dense grids, formulas, keyboard operations and precise motion.
5. Research Oracle - narrative, filings, citation and comparison focus.
6. Signal Access - maximum legibility, visible boundaries and minimal movement.

## Component grammar

Every route uses reusable global chrome, navigation groups, curved heroes, modular bento panels, data grids, evidence chips, forms, charts, empty/error/loading states and contextual actions. Variations are driven by content priority rather than arbitrary decoration.

## Text polarity

- Light text: deepest burgundy, graphite, midnight and approved dark overlays.
- Dark text: white, porcelain, soft rose and pale analytical canvases.
- Secondary text must retain readable contrast and cannot be used to hide important source, freshness, risk or simulation labels.

## Buttons

Primary buttons use high-contrast porcelain or gradient emphasis depending on surface. Secondary buttons use soft rose/porcelain. Ghost buttons remain visible against dark surfaces. All have hover, focus-visible, pressed, disabled and reduced-motion behaviour.

## Motion

Motion clarifies hierarchy, continuity and state changes. Primary actions may use a bounded magnetic response and shimmer; panels may reveal progressively; cards may use subtle tilt; routes transition through a short blur/reveal; charts animate data entry. The reduced-motion profile removes transforms and shortens or eliminates animation.

## Charting

The packaged chart adapter supports candlesticks, volume and SMA through an optional TradingView Lightweight Charts interface and a local SVG fallback. Binance and CoinDCX adapters are public and read-only. Advanced/private chart libraries and commercial market-data contracts are not redistributed.
