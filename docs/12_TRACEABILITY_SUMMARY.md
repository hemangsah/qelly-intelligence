## Classification rules

- **Implemented local foundation** means executable deterministic code, UI, API or validation evidence exists in the package.
- **Partial local contract** means architecture, schema or limited local behavior exists, but production dependencies or operational completeness are absent.
- **Production deferred** means the capability is deliberately disabled or unimplemented because it requires production identity, infrastructure, licensed data, external delivery, regulatory review, distributed durability or financial/custody controls.

No traceability status should be interpreted as a claim of production certification, commercial completeness or regulatory approval.
