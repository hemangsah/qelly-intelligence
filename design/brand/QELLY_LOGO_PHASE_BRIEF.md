# Qelly Logo-First Brand Phase Brief

## Authority

The user-supplied selected Qelly logo is the authoritative official brand mark for this phase. The identity consists of the burgundy Q ring and tail, the controlled rising trajectory, the green lower-left node, the red upper-right node and the QELLY wordmark.

Optical production refinements may improve alignment, curve smoothness, stroke consistency, node geometry, spacing and small-size legibility. They must not materially redesign or replace the approved mark.

## Allowed scope

- production vector reconstruction and governed logo variants;
- semantic brand tokens and usage rules;
- opening experience and reduced-motion alternative;
- premium logo-first homepage hero;
- desktop, collapsed and mobile shell branding;
- command-palette and authentication branding;
- route, chart and table loading states;
- empty, offline and recoverable-error states;
- favicon, Apple touch icon, PWA, maskable, pinned-tab and social-preview assets;
- light, dark and monochrome logo rules across all approved themes;
- logo motion specification;
- Figma brand-foundation generators and handoff evidence;
- automated logo governance, browser validation and visual-review artifact generation.

## Prohibited scope

- no unrelated frontend or backend features;
- no trading execution, wallets, exchanges, billing or portfolio-logic changes;
- no market-ingestion or unrelated database changes;
- no weakening of CI, CodeQL, security, production identity, APIs, schemas, persistence or tenant isolation;
- no replacement of IBM Plex;
- no arbitrary recolouring of the official mark;
- no public deployment from this branch;
- no merge or ready-for-review transition before explicit visual approval.

## Delivery gate

The branch must produce `qelly-logo-first-brand-system-review.zip` with a runnable compiled preview, screenshots, Figma-derived evidence, validation reports, checksums and a manifest. Green automation is not visual approval. The draft pull request must stop for Hemang Sah's visual decision.
