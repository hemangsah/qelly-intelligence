5. **Derivatives frontend** — only after contracts, deterministic fixtures, methodology, and tests exist.
6. **Research and AI governance** — citations, contradiction, source quality, Ask Qelly/Explain This Move evidence requirements, and export.
7. **Portfolio/workspaces** — exposure, scenarios, provenance, compare tray persistence, saved views, and alert evidence.
8. **Backend/provider expansion** — licensed adapters, PostgreSQL migrations, jobs, reconciliation, retention, and audit.
9. **Accessibility/performance** — real-browser regression, route chunks, virtualization, cancellation, budgets, zoom, screen-reader, and device testing.
10. **Preview and production release** — staging dependencies, security/legal/licensing review, load/soak/rollback exercises, and public deployment verification.

Each item remains a focused branch and reviewed PR. Missing destinations are not placeholder routes.
