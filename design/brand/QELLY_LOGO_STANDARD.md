# Qelly Logo Standard

The user-selected Qelly mark is authoritative. The production reconstruction preserves the burgundy Q ring and tail, rising intelligence trajectory, green and red decision nodes, and QELLY wordmark.

## Clear space and minimum size
- Clear space: at least half the symbol diameter.
- Horizontal lockup: minimum 120 px.
- Symbol: minimum 16 px; use the small-size variant below 24 px.
- Do not crop the Q tail or trajectory nodes.

## Approved variants
Primary/light, inverted/dark, monochrome dark, monochrome light, high contrast, compact, symbol and small-size symbol.

## Prohibited use
No stretching, arbitrary recoloring, heavy glow, noisy backgrounds, distorted Q geometry, excessive animation, or unreadable small wordmarks.
